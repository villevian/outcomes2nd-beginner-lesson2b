Як це працює?
В англійській є пари прикметників: -ed і -ing. Вони утворюються від одного дієслова, але означають різне.

-ed → як почувається людина

I am bored. — Мені нудно.
She feels excited. — Вона в захваті.
He was confused. — Він розгубився.

-ing → яка є ситуація / річ

The film is boring. — Фільм нудний.
The news is exciting. — Новини захопливі.
The task is confusing. — Завдання незрозуміле.

Просте правило:
Запитай себе: хто відчуває? → -ed  ·  що викликає відчуття? → -ing

Типова помилка:
❌ The film was bored. — фільм не може нудьгувати!
✅ The film was boring. I was bored.

Зверни увагу в діалозі:
Mia каже: "I'm exhausting at work" — і сама виправляє: "I mean — exhausted."
Exhausting = ти виснажуєш інших. Exhausted = ти сам виснажений.

Jake: Hey, you look tired. Everything okay?

Mia: Honestly? I'm exhausting → exhausted at work lately. I mean — exhausted. Sorry. Yeah, I'm really thinking about leaving.

Jake: Wait, seriously? But you love that job. You always talk about how interesting it is.

Mia: I used to love it. But right now I'm working on this project that never ends. My manager keeps changing everything. It's so frustrating.

Jake: What do you usually do there, again? You design interfaces, right?

Mia: Yeah, normally I design mobile apps and work with the dev team. It's actually exciting work. But at the moment my boss is giving all the creative tasks to a new guy and I'm just doing reports. I feel so bored.

Jake: That sounds annoying. So what are you thinking?

Mia: I'm applying to two companies right now. One is a small startup — they make educational apps. The other one is a big tech firm.

Jake: Oh interesting. Which one do you prefer?

Mia: The startup seems more exciting but the salary is lower. The big company is more stable. I'm so confused, honestly.

Jake: Okay, so — what do you usually look for in a job? Like, what's important to you?

Mia: Hmm. I need creative freedom. And good people around me. Money matters too, but it's not everything.

Jake: Then the startup sounds right for you. You get bored easily when work is boring — I know you.

Mia: Ha. True. My current job is so boring right now that I'm literally falling asleep in meetings.

Jake: Do it. Apply properly, prepare well. You're talented. Don't stay somewhere that's making you miserable.

Mia: You're right. Actually — are you looking for a new job too? You seem stressed lately.

Jake: Me? No, I love my job. Right now I'm working on something really challenging but I find it motivating, not tiring. I'm lucky, I think.

Mia: So annoying. I'm jealous.

Jake: (laughs) Apply to the startup. Seriously.
